Who I Am

Hi! I'm Eva — a data analyst who genuinely loves finding the story hidden inside a messy spreadsheet. I grew up in Taiwan, studied Finance & International Business at Fu Jen Catholic University, and spent a year interning at Shopee where I got hooked on using data to drive marketing and e-commerce decisions.


After graduating, I took a leap and moved to Australia on a working holiday — a year of broadening my perspective, improving my English, and realising I wanted to go deeper into the data world. That clarity pushed me to sit the IELTS, get accepted into QUT's Master of IT (Data Science), and dive headfirst into machine learning, databases, and analytics.


During my master's, I worked as an Assistant Data Analyst at QUT, building Power BI dashboards and SQL pipelines for real institutional decisions. I also collaborated with the Queensland Government on a deep learning project, and won a health-tech hackathon along the way.


What makes me a bit different is my cross-domain background: I understand the language of education, e-commerce, and finance, which means I can ask the right business questions before analysing the data. I'm passionate about data storytelling, machine learning, and AI technologies, and I enjoy continuously learning and applying new knowledge to solve real-world problems.
